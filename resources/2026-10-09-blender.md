## What it is

Blender is a free, open-source 3D creation suite covering modeling, animation, rendering, and video editing. Coordinated by the Blender Foundation, licensed under the GPL, free for any purpose including commercial work.

## Why it matters

Soren produces branded AI video work (Girona Splits, Girona Somebodies). Blender is the capable no-cost option for 3D and motion work behind that kind of creative, with a large community and no license fee.
